Adobo

Adobo is one of the most popular Filipino dishes, known for its savory, tangy, and garlicky flavor. It is traditionally made by simmering meat in vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns.

Ingredients

  • Pork or chicken
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic
  • Bay leaves
  • Whole peppercorns

Cooking Method

Marinate the meat in soy sauce and garlic. Simmer with vinegar, bay leaves, and peppercorns until tender. Reduce the sauce and lightly brown the meat before serving.
