Pakbet

Pakbet (or Pinakbet) is a traditional Filipino vegetable dish from Northern Luzon. It is made with mixed vegetables cooked in shrimp paste, creating a savory and earthy flavor.

Ingredients

  • Eggplant
  • Bitter melon (ampalaya)
  • Squash
  • Okra
  • String beans
  • Shrimp paste (bagoong)

Cooking Method

Sauté shrimp paste with onions and tomatoes. Add vegetables and simmer gently until tender. Avoid over-stirring to keep the vegetables intact and flavorful.
