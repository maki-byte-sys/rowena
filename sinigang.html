Sinigang

Sinigang is a classic Filipino sour soup usually cooked with pork, shrimp, or fish. Its signature sour taste comes from tamarind and is balanced with vegetables like kangkong, radish, and eggplant.

Ingredients

  • Pork or shrimp
  • Tamarind mix
  • Kangkong
  • Radish
  • Tomatoes

Cooking Method

Boil the meat until tender, add vegetables, mix in tamarind, season with salt or fish sauce, and simmer until cooked.
