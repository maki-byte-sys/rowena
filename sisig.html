Sisig

Sisig is a popular Filipino dish made from chopped pork face and ears, seasoned with calamansi, onions, and chili peppers. It is traditionally served sizzling and enjoyed as both a main dish and a pulutan.

Ingredients

  • Pork face or pork belly
  • Calamansi or lemon juice
  • Onion
  • Chili peppers
  • Soy sauce
  • Black pepper

Cooking Method

Boil the pork until tender, then grill or pan-fry until crispy. Chop finely and mix with calamansi juice, onions, chili, and seasonings. Serve hot on a sizzling plate.
