Kinilaw

Kinilaw is a traditional Filipino dish made with fresh raw fish cured in vinegar or citrus juice. Often compared to ceviche, it is flavored with onions, ginger, chili, and sometimes coconut milk.

Ingredients

  • Fresh raw fish (tuna or tanigue)
  • Vinegar or calamansi juice
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Chili peppers
  • Salt and pepper

Cooking Method

Clean and cube the fish, then soak briefly in vinegar or citrus juice. Drain, mix with onions, ginger, chili, and seasonings. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving fresh.
